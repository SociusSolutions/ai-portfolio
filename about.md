I build AI systems and automation, in two quite different settings.

**In a corporate setting**, cloud security and knowledge engineering on Azure: a
platform that inventories and continuously assesses a multi-tenant enterprise identity
estate against a security baseline, and a retrieval pipeline that turns a decades-deep
document archive into something answerable, with a measured evaluation harness rather
than a demo.

**In my own business**, a Brazilian jiu-jitsu academy in Winnipeg, I built and operate
the system that runs it — accounting, membership, lead generation, communications,
curriculum and reporting — as a single version-controlled operating system with agent
skills, MCP integrations and generated reporting.

The second setting is the reason the first one goes well. Running the business means I
am the person who gets paged when the automation is wrong, who has to explain a bad
number to an accountant, and who watches a member's experience degrade if a
classification is off. That closes a feedback loop most engineering work never closes,
and it has made me considerably more conservative about where a language model belongs
and considerably more aggressive about measuring the places it does.

## What I am looking for

Work where the interesting problem is making AI reliable in production rather than
demonstrating that it is possible — retrieval you can put a number on, agents with tool
surfaces that cannot cause harm by accident, automation that a business actually depends
on. Senior individual-contributor engineering, architecture, or AI-focused consulting.

## Honest notes on this portfolio

A portfolio should be auditable, so:

- **Corporate work is described at capability level only.** The client, its data, its
  environment and the measured results are withheld. What is described is the
  architecture class, the problem, and my approach. Detail is available on request,
  subject to the engagement terms.
- **Every system here is one I designed and built**, solo except the two corporate
  projects, where I was an engineer on the delivering team and say so.
- **Open-source tools I operate but did not write** are not listed as my work. In the
  security role that includes the multi-tenant administration and identity-assessment
  tooling common to the field; I run and extend them, I did not author them.
- **Where a project has no AI in it, I say so.** The
  [garage-sale map]({{ '/projects/rummage-map/' | relative_url }}) has no model in the
  request path, deliberately, and its page explains why.
- **The "what I'd do differently" section on every project is real.** It is the section
  I would want to read.
